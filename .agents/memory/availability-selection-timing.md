---
name: Early team drafting
description: User-required timing for opening team selection while availability replies are still arriving.
---

Clubs must be able to start drafting their teams as soon as they send the availability request. Do not make them wait for the cut-off or for all players to respond.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested this timing.

**How to apply:** Keep draft teams editable while responses are coming in, retaining the existing permissions and availability warnings. Repeat sends and cut-off processing must preserve selection edits rather than rebuilding teams over them. Existing already-sent rounds must also allow drafting without requiring another request send.
