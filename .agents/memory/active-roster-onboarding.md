---
name: Active roster without participant reports
description: User-required onboarding and manual roster management for availability and selection.
---

When a new tenant signs up after starting its season, players who have already played a game that season should be active. Admins and captains must be able to activate a previous player or create a new active player without repeatedly uploading the PlayHQ participant report.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested that clubs not depend on repeated participant-report uploads to maintain their active roster.

**How to apply:** Use the current season's recorded club appearances, not all historical players or opponents. Preserve staff activity decisions and existing identities on repeated additions or later imports. A seeded name does not supply contact details; missing contacts must not be invented, and junior availability messages must go through guardians.
